Carbon: 24 authored scenarios, with different replacement fractions chosen by the combined optimizer. Neither experiment measures taste preservation.

How EcoSwap makes a suggestion

The engine calculates ingredient contributions, ranks training-supported replacements, and searches partial-replacement options. It can abstain. The scores describe evidence and a modelling preference; they do not predict a percentage of taste retained.

How the score works

Candidate evidence combines log-scaled substitution frequency (weight __W_SUPPORT__), average context association (__W_CONTEXT__), and chemical Jaccard overlap (up to __W_CHEMISTRY__, downweighted for sparse profiles). The base weights are manually chosen to include flavour-compound evidence. Missing terms are omitted and available weights normalized.

The option objective is α × recipe carbon reduction − (1 − α) × fraction² × (1 − evidence). This convex change penalty is an explicit heuristic, not a fitted sensory model. No change is always considered.

Data provenance and limitations

AGRIBALYSE 3.2: 2,451 API records retrieved; 40 explicitly mapped. FlavorGraph: 32 of the 40 ingredients have chemical profiles. GISMo: 49,044 training records; directed candidate support learned only from training. Environmental factors are French averages, and several food-state/ingredient aliases are proxies. Mapping notes and source snapshots are in the project.

These 24 scenarios were authored for the demo. RecipeEmissions was inspected but rejected as a quantity source after finding suspicious unit conversions. Nutrition, water use, actual cooking energy and unchanged taste are not established by this model.

Taste pilot: pending. The package includes a protocol and an empty rating sheet. No people have cooked or rated these adaptations as part of this implementation.
